Artificial intelligence is a discipline within computer science focused on creating intelligent agents that perceive their environment and take actions to achieve specific goals.

Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that empowers computer systems to learn patterns and representations from historical data rather than being explicitly programmed with static rules.

Neural networks are computational models inspired by biological neural circuits in animal brains. A neural network consists of layers of interconnected neurons, where each connection transmits a real-valued signal that is modulated by a learnable parameter called a weight and shifted by a bias parameter.

Deep learning architectures stack multiple hierarchical layers between input and output representations. Through non-linear activation functions such as ReLU, GELU, Sigmoid, and Softmax, deep networks approximate complex high-dimensional non-linear functions.

Optimization algorithms find the parameter values that minimize a scalar cost or loss function. Gradient descent computes the partial derivatives of the loss with respect to all parameters using the chain rule of calculus in a process called backpropagation.

Attention mechanisms allow neural networks to dynamically focus on different parts of an input sequence regardless of distance. In scaled dot-product attention, queries, keys, and values interact through dot products to calculate attention weight distributions.

Transformers replace recurrent and convolutional architectures with multi-head self-attention and position-wise feed-forward networks, enabling massive parallelization across tokens during training.

Language models learn joint probability distributions over token sequences. In causal language modeling, the network receives previous context tokens and predicts the probability distribution over vocabulary tokens for the next position.

Computer vision empowers systems to extract visual information from images and video arrays. Convolutional filters detect spatial hierarchies of local visual features including edges, textures, motifs, and complex shapes.

Optical character recognition pipelines transform visual text contained in document photographs or scans into machine-readable digital strings through image binarization, noise reduction, contour detection, and sequence recognition.

MyAI is an integrated multimodal artificial intelligence platform combining language models, computer vision analysis, image synthesis, and character recognition into a unified software service.
